## 🇬🇧 English

### 1. Overview
This Privacy Policy explains how **Ada 101 Okey - İnternetsiz** handles user data. We are committed to protecting user privacy.

### 2. Information Collection and Use
**Ada 101 Okey - İnternetsiz does NOT collect, store, or share any personal information.**
- The game operates completely **offline**.
- No account registration or login is required.
- We do not access or collect location data, device identifiers (GAID, IDFA), contacts, or network data.
- No gameplay analytics or usage metrics are transmitted to any servers.

### 3. Local Storage
Game preferences such as chosen avatar and sound/music toggle states are stored strictly locally on the user's device. This data never leaves the device.

### 4. Advertisements and Third-Party SDKs
This application contains **NO advertisements**. We do not use any third-party ad networks, tracking technologies, or analytics SDKs.

### 5. Children's Privacy
Because we collect no personal data whatsoever, our application fully complies with children's privacy protection standards (COPPA, GDPR).
